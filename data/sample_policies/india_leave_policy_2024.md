# India Annual Leave Policy 2024 (SUPERSEDED)

## Clause 1: Scope & Eligibility
This policy applies to all regular employees in India for the 2024 calendar year. Notice: This version was superseded on 1 January 2025 by the India Annual & Statutory Leave Policy 2025.

## Clause 2: Annual Leave Accrual
Employees accrue Annual Leave at 1.5 working days per completed month of active service, providing 18 days per calendar year.

## Clause 4: Annual Leave Carryover (Historical Rule)
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into 2025. All other unused days are forfeited on 31 December 2024.
