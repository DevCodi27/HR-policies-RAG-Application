# Global Grade Band Entitlement Matrix 2025

## Clause 1: Grade Band Architecture
The organization classifies roles across 7 standardized seniority grade bands across all geographic entities:
- Grade 1 to Grade 2: Associate & Operational Staff
- Grade 3 to Grade 4: Professional & Senior Specialist Staff
- Grade 5: Lead & Principal Staff / Engineering Manager
- Grade 6: Director & Senior Director Staff
- Grade 7: Vice President & Executive Leadership

## Clause 2: Executive Wellness Days
Employees in Grade Band 5, Grade Band 6, and Grade Band 7 are entitled to 3 additional paid Executive Wellness Days per calendar year, taken at the employee's discretion in addition to standard statutory annual leave, to support sustainable work-life balance and mental well-being. Executive Wellness Days cannot be encashed or carried forward.

## Clause 3: Business Travel Class Policy
- Grades 1 through 4 travel Economy Class on all domestic and international flights.
- Grades 5 through 7 are eligible for Business Class on international flights exceeding 6 continuous hours of scheduled flight time.
