# India Annual & Statutory Leave Policy 2025

## Clause 1: Scope & Eligibility
This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees contracted under the India legal operating entity across all job levels and grade bands (Grade 1 through Grade 7). Interns and contractors are governed under separate service contracts.

## Clause 2: Annual Leave Accrual
Full-time employees accrue Annual Leave at the rate of 1.75 working days per completed calendar month of active service, resulting in a total entitlement of 21 working days per calendar year. Accrual begins on the first day of employment and is credited at the end of each calendar month.

## Clause 3: Sick & Casual Leave Entitlement
All employees in the India entity are credited with 12 days of paid Sick and Casual Leave on January 1 of each calendar year (pro-rated for new hires joining mid-year). Sick leave lasting more than three consecutive working days requires a valid medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

## Clause 4: Annual Leave Carryover & Expiration
Employees are encouraged to utilize their accrued annual leave within the calendar year. A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any carried-forward leave exceeding 8 days that remains unapproved by December 31 will automatically lapse without financial encashment. Carried-forward days must be utilized by March 31 of the new year.

## Clause 5: Compassionate & Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 3 consecutive working days of paid Compassionate Leave in the unfortunate event of the death or critical illness of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling). Additional discretionary days require prior written approval from the assigned Lead HRBP.

## Clause 6: Negative Leave Balance & Advance Borrowing
Employees with insufficient accrued leave may request to borrow up to a maximum of 5 unaccrued annual leave days in advance, subject to written approval from their Department Head and People Operations. In the event of separation, any negative leave balance will be recovered from the employee's final settlement.
