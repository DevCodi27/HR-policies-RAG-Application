# UK Annual Leave & Sickness Absence Policy 2025

## Clause 1: Scope & Statutory Framework
This policy applies to all employees operating under a United Kingdom employment contract across all grade bands (Grade 1 through Grade 7). This policy complies with the UK Working Time Regulations 1998 and relevant statutory employment legislation.

## Clause 2: Statutory Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time UK employees are entitled to 28 days (5.6 weeks) of paid annual leave per holiday year (1 January to 31 December). This entitlement is inclusive of the standard 8 UK Bank and Public Holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted weekly working hours.

## Clause 3: Sickness Absence & Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)
Employees unable to attend work due to illness must notify their line manager by 09:30 AM on the first day of absence. For absences up to 7 consecutive calendar days, employees may self-certify. For absences exceeding 7 calendar days, a Statement of Fitness for Work (Fit Note) from a GP or qualified healthcare provider is mandatory. Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) is paid in accordance with UK statutory qualifying conditions from the fourth qualifying day of absence.

## Clause 4: Annual Leave Carryover
Under the UK Working Time Regulations, 20 days of statutory leave must be taken within the leave year. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused days of contractual leave into the following holiday year, provided they obtain written agreement from their People Manager prior to 1 December. All carried-over days must be utilized within the first 6 months of the subsequent holiday year.

## Clause 5: Compassionate & Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to up to 5 working days of paid Compassionate Leave upon the death or life-threatening illness of an immediate family member. Statutory Parental Bereavement Leave (Jack's Law) of two weeks paid leave is provided to eligible parents who lose a child under the age of 18 or suffer a stillbirth after 24 weeks of pregnancy.
